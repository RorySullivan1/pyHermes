{# NOTES — a field's copy with its footnote markers rendered (#182). `marked(parts, raw)` walks what `apparatus.split_markers` cut: a run of copy prints as it always did, escaped unless the field is one of the blessed raw-HTML five, and a marker prints as a linked superscript. On paper the note itself follows the marker in a span the paged skeleton floats to the sheet foot (`float: footnote`, a WeasyPrint feature); an email collects its notes as endnotes instead, so the span is not emitted there. The number is the document's, never a CSS counter, so the text part prints the same one.

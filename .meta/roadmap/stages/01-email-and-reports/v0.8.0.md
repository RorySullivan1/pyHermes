## Goals
- Roadmap epic 1.3: three standard emails as parameterised products: daily note, weekly
  wrap, and a research-report announcement with the PDF attached.

## Objectives / acceptance
- Each template is a `Report` that renders from parameters and data alone, with no
  hand-built section tree in the caller.
- Each joins the gallery and has a golden; each stays under the 90 KB warning at its
  fixture's size.
- The announcement's message stays inside the 20 MB wire budget with its PDF rendered under
  `SCREEN`.

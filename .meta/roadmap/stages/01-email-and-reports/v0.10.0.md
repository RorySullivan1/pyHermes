status: planned

## Goals
- Roadmap epic 1.6: every run writes a manifest: parameters, data hashes, the rendered
  bytes' hash, who it went to and when. The compliance answer to "what was sent?".

## Objectives / acceptance
- Given a manifest, the same version of pyHermes re-renders bytes whose hash matches it.
- A send records each recipient's outcome in the manifest, including failures.
- The manifest format is documented and versioned.

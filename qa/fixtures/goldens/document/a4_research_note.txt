Summary
-------

Momentum has earned a premium in every market studied since the first evidence
(Jegadeesh and Titman 1993), and it survives the four-factor model (Carhart
1997). Its cost is the momentum crash (Daniel and Moskowitz 2016).[1]

------------------------------------------------------------------------------

KEY TAKEAWAYS

The premium survives trading costs at moderate turnover, and the crash risk is
what a position should be sized to.

------------------------------------------------------------------------------


Data and Method
---------------

Stocks are ranked each month on their return over the prior twelve months,
skipping the most recent (Jegadeesh and Titman 1993). The winner-minus-loser

Glossary
--------

Momentum: The tendency of recent winners to keep outperforming recent losers.

Momentum crash: A sudden reversal in which past losers sharply outperform.

Turnover: The share of a portfolio traded each month to keep it on its signal.

Winner-minus-loser: A portfolio long the top decile of past returns and short
the bottom decile.

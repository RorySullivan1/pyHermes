## Decisions and why
- **The component takes bytes; the extra renders.** The stub put `MathBlock(latex)` in `components.py`
  rendering through matplotlib. `test_no_core_module_imports_an_optional_backend_or_the_adapters`
  forbids it, and `data-layer.md` already records why there is no `DataTable.from_frame`. The
  epic follows the `chart_from_figure` pair: `MathBlock(image, latex)` in the builder,
  `math_block(latex, ...)` in `svc/math`.
- **The theme cannot recolour a picture.** Pixels are fixed at construction, the theme binds at
  render. Colour and size are the adapter's inputs from the theme the caller passes; stated as a
  limitation, not hidden.
- **One render covers both media.** `scale=4` gives 384 dpi at 96 px/in, over `print_dpi`; the
  `PdfProfile` caps downward only, so `SCREEN` downsamples and `PRINT` keeps it. About 5 KB each.
- **No golden may depend on matplotlib's fonts.** Fixture images are `solid_png`; `tests/test_math.py`
  pins render-twice equality and pixel dimensions within one px, never bytes.
- **Multi-line is a stacked image, not `aligned`.** mathtext refuses `\begin{aligned}` and a bare `\\`;
  a newline between `$…$` runs renders one image. `&` alignment is out, with the reason.
- **No list of equations.** The apparatus has no list of exhibits; `Contents` lists sections only.

## What was measured (matplotlib 3.11.2, this machine)
- Deterministic bytes in-process; five fontsets give five byte streams and five pixel boxes.
- Refused: `\begin{aligned}`, `\\`, `\le` (`\leq` works), `\displaystyle`, `\limits`, `\underbrace`, `\&`.
- Risk-budget equation at 14px: 82×24 px at 1×, 247×71 at 3×, 329×94 at 4×; 3.6 KB at 3×.
- A parse error is a `ValueError` whose last line names the symbol and column, raised inside `savefig`.

## What surprised
- `\le` is unknown to mathtext while `\leq` is fine; a probe using `\le` failed before the multi-line
  test ran. Probe each symbol the docs will list.
- The `fill-self` sentence rewrite from #217 recurred exactly as recorded; printing the diff this time
  made the two-line change visible and the five updates went once.
